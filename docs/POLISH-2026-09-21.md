# Landing-page polish — 21 Sep 2026

Sam: "Right now it still feels a bit AI generated, even though it's much better
than I thought it would be. I want this to noticeably feel different and refined."
Two pages: the pitch (`/`) and the Coffeeholics splash (`/coffeeholics`). Planned by
Fable 5.1, implemented by Opus 5 workers, verified by Fable.

This is **refinement, not redesign**. The world stays: Gilroy, the maroon dark field on
the pitch, Coffeeholics' purple on the light splash, the nine tokens, the glass panel.
What changes is **composition** — what gets a box, what sits open on the ground, what
is large, what is small, and how the desktop uses its width.

---

## 1. Diagnosis — what reads as "AI generated"

Measured against the references (Mobbin: Shopify Plus, Railway, Dollar Shave Club,
Superpower, sweetgreen, UGLYCASH) and the craft floor:

1. **Every section is the same rounded panel.** Same 16px radius, same hairline, same
   16px padding, stacked. Nothing sits open on the ground; nothing bleeds. The
   references box at most one thing per page.
2. **The icon-tile + bold + grey-subline row, everywhere.** Splash benefits, pitch
   benefit cards, pop-up rows, modal steps. On the pitch desktop it is *four equal
   cards of icon + heading + text*, which is the textbook tell.
3. **Flat type.** One 32px H1, then everything at 13–17px. The number that *is* the
   product ($5, $4.99, $76.25) is never a display moment. The references put the
   figure at 2–3× the headline.
4. **Desktop is a widened phone.** The splash at 1440 is a 720px column with nothing
   either side; its merchant photograph is a 168px strip. The pitch hero's left column
   holds eleven elements in 440px.
5. **Uppercase tracked eyebrows on every panel** ("EVERY WEEK, NOT ONCE", "WHAT YOU
   GET", "APPLIES TO", "WHAT YOU'D SAVE"). One is a label; four is a tic.
6. **Three stacked full-width pills** in the pitch hero on a phone (Get early access,
   How it works, Sign in). One filled action per view; the rest are links.
7. **Small text below the mobile floor.** `.t-compact` is 13px and carries real
   sentences (seat line, one-time note, the alcohol note). Body is 15.

## 2. Shared moves — done by Fable before workers start

In `src/styles/tokens.css` only. Workers must not edit tokens.css or layout.css.

- **Type ramp gains two display steps and a section head:**
  - `.t-hero` — 36px phone / 56px from 1024. 700, −.035em, line 1.02. The page H1.
  - `.t-figure` — 48px phone / 64px from 1024. `--font-heavy`, tabular, −.03em, line
    0.95. **The one display moment per surface**: the dollar figure.
  - `.t-section` — 22px, 700, −.02em, sentence case. Replaces `.t-caption` wherever a
    label *heads a section* rather than a list.
- **Mobile floor raised:** `.t-body` 15→16, `.t-compact` 13→14, `.t-lead` 16→17.
  `.t-caption` stays 11px and is now legal **only** on a list label inside a panel or
  on a chip; never as a section heading.
- Section gap on phone stays `--s-3xl` (40); desktop `--s-4xl` (64) stays.

The rule for workers: **a `.t-caption` above content is a smell. Use `.t-section`.**
