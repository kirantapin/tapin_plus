# Landing-page polish — 21 Sep 2026

Sam: "Right now it still feels a bit AI generated, even though it's much better
than I thought it would be. I want this to noticeably feel different and refined."
Two pages: the pitch (`/`) and the Coffeeholics splash (`/coffeeholics`). Planned by
Fable 5.1, implemented by Opus 5 workers, verified by Fable.

This is **refinement, not redesign**. The world stays: Gilroy, the maroon dark field on
the pitch, Coffeeholics' purple on the light splash, the nine tokens, the glass panel.
What changes is **composition** — what gets a box, what sits open on the ground, what
is large, what is small, and how the desktop uses its width.

## 1. Diagnosis — what reads as "AI generated"

Measured against the references (Mobbin: Shopify Plus, Railway, Dollar Shave Club,
Superpower, sweetgreen, UGLYCASH) and the craft floor:

1. **Every section is the same rounded panel.** Same 16px radius, same hairline, same
   16px padding, stacked. Nothing sits open on the ground; nothing bleeds. The
   references box at most one thing per page.
2. **The icon-tile + bold + grey-subline row, everywhere.** Splash benefits, pitch
   benefit cards, pop-up rows, modal steps. On the pitch desktop it is *four equal
   cards of icon + heading + text*, which is the textbook tell.
3. **Flat type.** One 32px H1, then everything at 13–17px. The number that *is* the
   product ($5, $4.99, $76.25) is never a display moment. The references put the
   figure at 2–3× the headline.
4. **Desktop is a widened phone.** The splash at 1440 is a 720px column with nothing
   either side; its merchant photograph is a 168px strip. The pitch hero's left column
   holds eleven elements in 440px.
5. **Uppercase tracked eyebrows on every panel** ("EVERY WEEK, NOT ONCE", "WHAT YOU
   GET", "APPLIES TO", "WHAT YOU'D SAVE"). One is a label; four is a tic.
6. **Three stacked full-width pills** in the pitch hero on a phone (Get early access,
   How it works, Sign in). One filled action per view; the rest are links.
7. **Small text below the mobile floor.** `.t-compact` is 13px and carries real
   sentences (seat line, one-time note, the alcohol note). Body is 15.

## 14. The pitch goes light below the hero (23 Sep)

Sam: "this all made me realize, I think this entire page needs to go light
mode instead of dark. We can have a dark mode header, but the stuff beneath
needs to be more legible."

### The shape

Two grounds, one edge. The announce bar, the hero band (mosaic, glass panel,
lockup, H1, lead, proof, rail, price, actions) stay exactly as they are: the
dark world is the header. Everything under the hero band — What you get,
Applies to and its note, What you'd save, the guarantee, the legal links —
sits on the **light ramp** (`styles/light.css`: page `#F5F2F3`, card white,
inner `#EFEAEC`, the ink ramp inverted). The Coffeeholics page already lives
on a light ramp; this brings the pitch to the same room, with the hero as
its dark header.

### Build

1. **One wrapper, one attribute.** In `Pitch.tsx` everything after the hero
   band is inside `<div className="pitch-lit" data-lit="">`. `light.css`'s
   `[data-lit]` inversion does the tokens; the wrapper paints its own ground
   (`background: var(--page)`), bleeds to the viewport edges
   (`margin-inline: calc(50% - 50vw)`, padding back with the column's
   gutter) and runs to the page's foot (the body's dark gradient must never
   show below it — measure the last 1px of the page). The edge under the hero
   is straight and unadorned.
2. **The two docked bars follow the ground they float over.** `.top-cta`
   (desktop, appears once the hero is scrolled past) and `.sticky-cta` (phone
   pair) both float over the light region, so both carry `data-lit`: a white
   bar with dark ink, the maroon action unchanged (it is 9.19:1 on white).
   Check their hairline/shadow tokens resolve on the light ramp.
3. **Everything in the region reads through tokens.** Audit every rule
   pitch.css applies inside the region for a literal that assumes the dark
   field — white-alpha hairlines, `rgba(255,255,255,…)`, the glass `.panel`
   backdrop, `--on-maroon` used as "white text", `.bshot` plates (the `lit`
   prop on `PhotoCard` is now redundant on the pitch; leave the prop, stop
   passing it), the savings segmented track (`--press` chosen cell), the
   guarantee shield, `.cover` plates, the alcohol note, the section heads
   (`.t-section` is `--ink-1`, inverts by itself). Where a rule is scoped to
   the pitch and assumes dark, re-express it in tokens or scope a
   `[data-lit]` variant beside it. The desktop close band (`.pitch-decide`,
   `--subtle`) on the light ramp is white on `#F5F2F3`; if that reads as a
   panel-on-panel with the savings panel inside it, use `--inner` for the
   band or drop the band's fill.
4. **The photographs keep their edges.** Cards on the light ground get the
   light ramp's `--hair` and `--rest` shadow (light.css defines both), so a
   white plate on a `#F5F2F3` ground still separates.
5. **Nothing above the hero's foot changes**, and `/coffeeholics`, the
   pop-up, the checkout, the sign-in sheet are untouched.

### Acceptance

- 375 and 1440: every text node in the region ≥4.5:1 on its actual ground
  (the sweep in `scratchpad/accept.js` walks ancestors for the painted
  background); no literal colour outside tokens in any rule the region uses
  (grep the diff); nothing under 14px but the chips; no horizontal overflow;
  the docked bars white over the light region and hidden/absent over the
  hero as before; the page's last pixel row is `--page` light, not the dark
  gradient.
- The hero band's rendered HTML and its computed styles are unchanged.
