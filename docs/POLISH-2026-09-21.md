Sam: "Right now it still feels a bit AI generated, even though it's much better
than I thought it would be. I want this to noticeably feel different and refined."
Two pages: the pitch (`/`) and the Coffeeholics splash (`/coffeeholics`). Planned by
Fable 5.1, implemented by Opus 5 workers, verified by Fable.

This is **refinement, not redesign**. The world stays: Gilroy, the maroon dark field on
the pitch, Coffeeholics' purple on the light splash, the nine tokens, the glass panel.
What changes is **composition** — what gets a box, what sits open on the ground, what
is large, what is small, and how the desktop uses its width.

## 1. Diagnosis — what reads as "AI generated"

Measured against the references (Mobbin: Shopify Plus, Railway, Dollar Shave Club,
Superpower, sweetgreen, UGLYCASH) and the craft floor:

1. **Every section is the same rounded panel.** Same 16px radius, same hairline, same
   16px padding, stacked. Nothing sits open on the ground; nothing bleeds. The
   references box at most one thing per page.
2. **The icon-tile + bold + grey-subline row, everywhere.** Splash benefits, pitch
   benefit cards, pop-up rows, modal steps. On the pitch desktop it is *four equal
   cards of icon + heading + text*, which is the textbook tell.
3. **Flat type.** One 32px H1, then everything at 13–17px. The number that *is* the
   product ($5, $4.99, $76.25) is never a display moment. The references put the
   figure at 2–3× the headline.
4. **Desktop is a widened phone.** The splash at 1440 is a 720px column with nothing
   either side; its merchant photograph is a 168px strip. The pitch hero's left column
   holds eleven elements in 440px.
5. **Uppercase tracked eyebrows on every panel** ("EVERY WEEK, NOT ONCE", "WHAT YOU
   GET", "APPLIES TO", "WHAT YOU'D SAVE"). One is a label; four is a tic.
6. **Three stacked full-width pills** in the pitch hero on a phone (Get early access,
   How it works, Sign in). One filled action per view; the rest are links.
7. **Small text below the mobile floor.** `.t-compact` is 13px and carries real
   sentences (seat line, one-time note, the alcohol note). Body is 15.

## 25. The card's flight from the deck to the checkout (23 Sep, late)

Sam: "refine the animation from the how it works slides to the checkout for
this membership card? I think we'd want the cards to be the same and there
shouldn't be a jolt when it lands in its place at checkout — right now it
looks like the card changes when it lands."

### Why it jolts (read from `src/shell/cardFlight.ts`)

1. **Two different cards.** The deck's last slide shows the card at one size
   and the checkout at another (340, and 420 before §9 fixed it), so the
   flight has to scale, and it scales **non-uniformly** (`scale(sx, sy)` from
   the two rects' ratios). A card whose proportions change in flight is a
   card that "changes".
2. **The clone is the destination's DOM, not the origin's.** At takeoff the
   deck's card is swapped for a clone of the checkout's card squeezed to the
   deck's rect — any difference in type, padding or shadow between the two
   renderings pops at frame one.
3. **The landing rect is measured too early.** `to` is read in the mount
   effect while the checkout sheet may still be arriving (`sheetUp` /
   `rsPopIn`) and before fonts and the two-column grid settle, so the clone
   lands where the card *was*, and the 200ms crossfade reveals the real card
   somewhere slightly else — the jolt.

### The refinement

- **One card.** `TapInCard` at one width on both surfaces: 340 on the deck's
  slide 9 and at the checkout's close, phone and desktop (`min(340px,
  100% − 48px)` below 480). Same class, same shadow, same radius, same name
  field, so the flight is a **pure translate**, no scale.
- **Move the real card, not a clone.** FLIP on the destination card itself:
  after the sheet is still (`is-still` set for a flight, so the sheet does
  not animate its own entrance under the flight), after `document.fonts.ready`
  and two animation frames, measure `to`, apply `transform:
  translate(dx, dy)` from the stashed origin, and animate to `none` over
  820ms on the house expo-out. No clone, no crossfade, nothing to swap at
  landing. The stashed origin is measured with the deck's float/drift
  animations cancelled, as now.
- **Hold the rest back, as now** (`data-flight="in"` brings the sheet's
  other content up after the card lands); keep the reduced-motion bypass.
- If the origin rect is off-screen or the sizes still differ by more than
  2px (a resize between routes), skip the flight rather than scale.

### Acceptance

Desktop 1440 and phone 375: the deck's card and the checkout's card measure
the same width and height (report both); during the flight the card's box
keeps its size (sample the animated rect at 3 points); at landing the card's
final rect equals its settled rect with no second movement (compare the
